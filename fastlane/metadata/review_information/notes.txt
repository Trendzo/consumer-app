Trendzo is a fashion marketplace for India.

SIGNING IN
Sign-in is a phone number plus a 4-digit OTP. Please use the demo credentials provided: enter the
phone number, then the code 1234. This bypasses the SMS, which cannot be delivered outside India.

WHAT TO TRY
- Home: browse edits, categories and Steals.
- Tap any product to open it, choose a size and add to bag.
- Bag > Checkout: address, delivery method, then Review Order.
- Payments run through Razorpay. No real charge is made with the demo account.

PERMISSIONS
- Location is requested only when adding a delivery address, to pin it for delivery routing.
- Camera and photo library are requested only for virtual try-on, when you choose a photo.

NOTES
Express delivery, store pickup and Try and Buy are available where partner stores operate
(currently Mumbai). Outside those areas standard delivery is offered.
